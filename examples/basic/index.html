PanomNom.js examples

Each one loads a panorama with the library and shows it in 360°, flat, or as a download.

  • Street View from an idGoogleStreetViewLoader.load
  • Street View from a locationgetIdByLocation, with a map to click
  • Street View from a Maps URLgetIdFromURL
  • Street View depthGoogleStreetViewDepthLoader, in 3D
  • Photo sphere from an idGooglePhotoSphereLoader.load
  • Photo sphere from a Maps URLGooglePhotoSphereLoader.loadFromURL
  • Using the built bundlebuild/PanomNom.module.js
  • Floating Shiny KnotA complete example, with a map and more
